This pack includes the following:

✅ 34 animation for a knight
✅ Provided in formats:
   - Sprite Sheets (PNG)
✅ Looping and non-looping animations

List of effects:
- Idle (2) 
- Run (2)
- Jump (2)
- Mid air (2)
- Fall light (2)
- Fall heavy (2)
- Land (2)
- Roll (2)
- Weapon on
- Weapon off
- Attack (4)
- Heavy Attack (3)
- Weapon Art
- Buff (2)
- Parry (parry_start, parry, miss)
- Hurt (2)
- Death

Preview GIFs are included to demonstrate each animation.
